Export structured spreadsheets with full tender metadata, customer contacts, and direct Prozorro links for seamless CRM/ERP integration.

Receive periodic or scheduled email digests of matched tenders directly to your corporate inbox, plus device push alerts for urgent biddings (< 48 hours).
